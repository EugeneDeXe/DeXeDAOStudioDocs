# 0. Create DAO. Create and test for free. Testnet

Are you eager to unleash the full potential of your DAO, but worried about the costs of gas on the blockchain? Fear not! We've got you covered. In this tutorial, we'll show you how to create and test your DAO using the BNB Smart Chain testnet, completely free of charge.

But first, let's talk about testnets. These special networks are designed to help developers, testers, and users like you try out their products without breaking the bank. And to make it even more convenient, there are faucets that give you free test tokens: including BNB — to use as payment for gas fees.

DeXe DAO Studio supports BNB Smart chain testnet as a test network. All features are available on the testnet as well as on the mainnet, we recommend testing your DAO, setup, quorums, voting delegations on the testnet first, and then using the mainnet and real assets. In this tutorial, we will show the process of getting BNB tokens via a faucet in the BSC network.

{% hint style="info" %}
tBNB has no financial value and cannot be traded at a real price, given its unlimited supply, which exclusively serves test and development purposes.
{% endhint %}

## How to use testnet in DeXe DAO Studio

Are you ready to take your decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) to the next level? But before you can do that, you need to test and refine your DAO's configuration, setup, and functionality. That's where the BNB Smart Chain testnet comes in.

In this tutorial, we'll show you how to use the testnet in DeXe DAO Studio to create and test your DAO without breaking the bank. With this step-by-step guide, you'll be able to:

* Get your hands on free BNB tokens using a faucet
* Set up your DAO's configuration, including quorums, voting delegations, and more
* Test your DAO on the BNB Smart Chain testnet
* Refine your DAO's functionality and performance

By following the provided guidance, you'll be able to create your own DAO or test an existing one in the BNB Smart Chain testnet environment, completely free of charge.

#### Step 1: Launch DeXe DAO Studio <a href="#docs-internal-guid-bbdf3553-7fff-936e-a66c-831b279ce5b1" id="docs-internal-guid-bbdf3553-7fff-936e-a66c-831b279ce5b1"></a>

Embark on your DAO creation journey by opening the DeXe DAO Studio. You can do this by following these simple steps:

* Head to [DeXe.io](http://dexe.io) and click on the "Launch App" button to open the DeXe DAO Studio web application. You'll see a homepage listing DAOs created by other users.&#x20;
* Alternatively, you can use a[ direct link](https://app.dexe.io/) to the web application, which is available on our website.

Once you've launched the web application, you'll be greeted with a homepage that showcases a list of DAOs created by other users. This is a great way to get inspiration and see what others have built with DeXe DAO Studio!



#### Step 2: Connect Your Crypto Wallet <a href="#docs-internal-guid-60a095dd-7fff-bd1d-f3fd-c367262d361f" id="docs-internal-guid-60a095dd-7fff-bd1d-f3fd-c367262d361f"></a>
