# 📖 Glossary

This glossary defines and explains terms and concepts you may encounter in the documentation for better understanding.

<table><thead><tr><th width="182">Term</th><th>Definition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>BAB Token, Soulbound token</strong></td><td>A Soulbound token is a non-transferable digital token that represents your personal achievements, affiliations, and experiences. Unlike regular NFTs, SBTs cannot be sold or traded, serving as verifiable proof of your skills, education, or community memberships in the emerging "Decentralized Society". DeXe DAO Studio provides an option to require the Binance Account Bound (BAB) token in addition to DAO governance token. This helps prevent sybil attacks from users with multiple accounts.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Decentralized Autonomous Organization, DAO</strong></td><td>A form of organization where decisions are made through proposals and voting based on rules encoded in smart contracts.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Delegate</strong></td><td>The user who receives delegated tokens for governance purposes.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="delegated-governance.md"><strong>Delegated governance</strong></a></td><td>Members can delegate governance tokens to any member of their DAO. It lets them transfer decision-making power to other DAO members or experts, allowing them to withdraw their tokens anytime. When delegating, tokens are locked in a smart contract, and only their owner can revoke them.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Delegator</strong></td><td>The user delegating assets to another user for governance purposes.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="https://app.dexe.io/dao/list/top"><strong>DeXe DAO Studio</strong></a></td><td>DeXe DAO Studio is a cutting-edge platform designed for the creation and management of Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs). It is a comprehensive solution for users to initiate and govern a DAO with ease. DeXe DAO Studio is built on top of the DeXe Protocol.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="https://www.dexe.network/"><strong>DeXe Protocol</strong></a></td><td>The DeXe Protocol is an ecosystem of 50+ smart contracts for building and governing effective DAOs.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Early Vote Completion</strong></td><td>A parameter that determines whether the main voting phase will end immediately after the quorum "for" or "against" is reached without waiting for the entire duration.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Execution Delay</strong></td><td>The time interval setting a buffer between the end of voting and proposal execution. The delay allows members to exit the DAO if they disagree with the proposal's outcome.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="experts.md"><strong>Experts</strong></a></td><td>Individuals or collective DAOs, recognized within the DAO community for their proficiency in specific fields. Their function is to ensure decisions are well-informed, technically viable, and advantageous for the ecosystem.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Gas Fees</strong></td><td>Gas fees in blockchain are commissions paid by users to compensate for the computing energy required to process and validate transactions on the network. The amount of gas fees can vary depending on the network's congestion and the complexity of the transaction.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="experts.md#global-experts"><strong>Global experts</strong></a></td><td>Can be individuals or collective entities, like <a href="experts.md#local-experts">Local Experts</a>. Nonlinear functions in this system prevent plutocracy — the concentration of power in the hands of a few — by slowing down the increase in voting power as a token balance grows.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="how-to-vote-in-dexe-dao-studio.md#docs-internal-guid-d66c56c7-7fff-f8ca-12de-e42c18273012"><strong>Off-chain voting</strong></a></td><td>The voting mechanism in which the results don't trigger any actions in the blockchain but are recorded in the backend. These proposals are essential for validating ideas or bringing up topics for discussion.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="how-to-vote-in-dexe-dao-studio.md#docs-internal-guid-92b3d38d-7fff-7ddb-73c5-0b53745aa7c3"><strong>On-chain voting</strong></a></td><td>A voting mechanism in which the results are recorded in the blockchain and trigger the execution of a transaction performing the function specified by the type of proposal. This could involve various actions such as assigning roles within the DAO, transferring funds from the treasury, token distribution, executing custom contracts, etc.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="operating-and-governing-your-dao/#proposal-lifecycle"><strong>Proposal</strong></a></td><td>A formal suggestion or idea presented by a member for consideration and potential approval by the community through voting. In DeXe DAO Studio is created using convenient templates.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="rewards-system.md#proposal-creation-rewards"><strong>Proposal Creation Rewards</strong></a></td><td>A fixed number of tokens will be given to the accepted proposal author as compensation for gas fees, incentivizing members to contribute ideas and take the initiative.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="how-to-vote-in-dexe-dao-studio.md#docs-internal-guid-2360a755-7fff-5a96-694a-f3bb52a2099f"><strong>Proposal Execution</strong></a></td><td>Triggering on-chain actions after a proposal is approved. Once the voting phase is finished and the proposal meets the conditions for approval, it can be executed via the relevant executor contract on-chain. This requires a transaction, though the DAO can set a small reward for executing proposals to cover transaction fees.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="operating-and-governing-your-dao/#quorums"><strong>Quorum</strong></a></td><td>The minimum amount of voting power; "Yes" – in favour; or "No" – opposed, to accept or decline a proposal respectively.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="rewards-system.md"><strong>Rewards System</strong></a></td><td>A flexible rewards system designed by DeXe DAO Studio to incentivize DAO members through bonuses for their significant contributions to the organization's activities. This approach encourages high-quality contributions and compensates members for the time and gas fees they incur.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="rewards-system.md#transaction-execution-rewards"><strong>Transaction Execution Rewards</strong></a></td><td>A fixed number of tokens will be given as rewards to users who execute accepted proposals, offsetting gas fees.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Total Value Governed, TVG</strong></td><td>Metric indicating in USD equivalent how many governance tokens (GT) are currently controlled by this DAO. Calculated as a sum of all governance tokens (GT) in the treasury, governance tokens (GT) delegated to this DAO and all governance tokens locked in other Dao's votings on behalf of this DAO.<br><br><em><strong>TVG</strong> = GT in treasury+GT received as delegations+GT locked in other DAOs votings</em></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Total Value Locked, TVL</strong></td><td><p>Metric indicating in USD equivalent how many tokens are locked in this DAO. Calculated as a sum of all assets in treasury, all governance tokens (GT) delegated by DAO, and all governance tokens locked in DAO member votings.<br></p><p><em><strong>TVL</strong> = Assets in treasury+GT delegated by DAO+GT locked in voting.</em></p></td></tr><tr><td></td><td></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Treasury</strong></td><td>A pool of funds managed collectively by a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO). Any actions with the Treasure funds are only available through on-chain transactions executed after the DAO members vote and accept the proposal. The Treasury is used to fund projects, operations, and investments decided upon through the DAO’s governance processes, where members vote on how funds should be allocated and used.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Validator Token</strong></td><td>Holding this token confirms the Validator's status in the DAO and allows them to participate in Validator voting.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="voting-logic.md#validators-voting"><strong>Validator voting</strong></a></td><td>Validators voting is an extra layer of security oversighting your DAO's governance through a secondary vote, which takes place after the member's voting phase. Validators use a separate Validator Token.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Validators</strong></td><td>Validators are trusted members who vote on proposals after the main community vote. They act like a board of directors, providing an additional safeguard against malicious proposals. If validator's voting is enabled in DAO, their approval is required for proposals to be executed. Validators also have special powers, such as proposing urgent changes without a full vote when needed to maintain the DAO.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Validators-Only Proposals</strong></td><td>Validators can conduct their own types of proposals without involving the entire DAO. These proposals are essential for managing the DAO's sustainability processes, which do not require full DAO participation to avoid slowing down operations.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="delegated-governance.md"><strong>Vote Delegation</strong></a></td><td>Allows DAO members to assign voting power to trusted experts or advisors in the community, facilitating better decision-making by leveraging their expertise. This also saves time and gas fees, while members retain the ability to withdraw delegated votes anytime.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="how-to-vote-in-dexe-dao-studio.md#voting-duration"><strong>Voting Duration</strong></a></td><td>The period during which the voting phase occurs.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Voting Efficiency Index, VEI</strong></td><td><p>In a <a href="voting-logic.md#nonlinear-voting">Meritocratic Non-linear voting system</a>, as the number of tokens on the balance increases, the function of calculating the voting power deliberately slows down its growth. For example, non-linear functions slow down the increase in voting power as a token balance grows.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="rewards-system.md#voting-rewards"><strong>Voting Rewards</strong></a></td><td>Is given to those who vote for proposals the DAO has accepted. The distribution of voting rewards is based on the percentage of tokens involved in voting and is allocated to members according to their voting power.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Voting rules</strong></td><td>DAO Voting Rules are predefined settings for different types of proposals fixed in protocol smart contracts. Can be modified by proposals and voting.</td></tr></tbody></table>
